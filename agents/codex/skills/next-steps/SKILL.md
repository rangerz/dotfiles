# Next Steps

When the turn naturally leaves distinct, useful follow-ups, append `接下來可以：` with three concise, numbered prompts by default. Include fewer only when fewer than three genuinely useful, distinct options remain; omit the section when none remain. Keep choices specific, grounded in the conversation, and easy to select by replying with a number. Skip trivial or complete answers; never add filler options or imply an action is approved or done.
